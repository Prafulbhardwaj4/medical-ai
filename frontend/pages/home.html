AI scribe · Growth plan and above

Your doctors talk. The note writes itself.

Doctors speak naturally in their own language, mixed or not. ScribeKare drafts the consultation note in many Indian languages. The doctor reads it, fixes what needs fixing and confirms.

  1. SpeakTalk to the patient as you always do, in your own language.
  2. DraftThe AI turns the conversation into a structured note and a prescription draft.
  3. ReviewEvery line is editable. Add, change or remove anything.
  4. ConfirmOnly the doctor's confirmation finalises the note. Then the prescription goes to the pharmacy, lab and bill.
The AI drafts. The doctor decides. It does not diagnose and it does not replace the doctor.

FAQ

Questions hospital owners ask

How do we get started?
Your hospital admin creates staff accounts for each role. You then load your tests and medicines from ready-made master lists: tick what you offer and type your prices, or upload a small Excel file. Setup by our team is part of the Enterprise plan, which is coming soon.
Is our data safe?
Access is role-based, so each person sees only what their job needs. Each hospital's data is kept separate from every other hospital. Important actions are recorded in an audit trail, and connections are encrypted with HTTPS.
Which plan should we choose?
Foundation covers OPD, lab, pharmacy, billing, online appointments and the patient portal. Growth adds admissions and ward management, the AI scribe and staff chat. Tell us about your hospital in the contact form and we will suggest one.
Which languages does it support?
The AI scribe understands Indian languages, even when a doctor mixes them in the same sentence. Hindi and English are supported today, and more Indian languages are planned.
How does the AI scribe work?
The doctor speaks during the consultation. The system drafts the note and a prescription. The doctor reviews, edits and confirms. The AI never finalises anything on its own. It is available on the Growth plan and above.
Can we switch plans later?
Yes. Tell us and we will move your hospital to the plan you need. Features of the new plan are available once the change is made.
Does it send WhatsApp messages automatically?
Yes. ScribeKare sends appointment reminders and tokens to patients on WhatsApp.
