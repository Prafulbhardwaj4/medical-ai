Everything under one roof

Every department, one connected system

No more switching between a registration register, a WhatsApp group for reports, and a diary for admissions. MedScribe runs the whole patient journey in one place.

📋

OPD — Registration to Prescription

Token-based registration, live queue management, consultation with the AI scribe, and a printable or WhatsApp-delivered prescription — all in one flow.

🛏️

IPD & Admissions

Ward and bed management, admission and discharge workflows, in-patient medication and test orders, and a running bill your billing team doesn't have to reconstruct by hand.

🧪

Pathology & Lab

Sample collection, result entry, verification and release — with reports available to the doctor and the patient the moment they're ready, no chasing required.

📅

Online Appointments

Patients book a slot from their phone; reception sees expected arrivals for the day, with automatic queue placement when they walk in.

💊

Pharmacy

Prescriptions flow straight to the pharmacy queue — dispense against stock, track batches and expiry, and keep billing in sync automatically.

🚑

Emergency Ward

Fast-track intake for emergency patients with live bed availability, so reception and the on-call doctor are never working from different information.
